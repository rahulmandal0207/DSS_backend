Source on GitHub

Detect driver fatigue on your own face

DSS watches the eyes, mouth and head, and decides from how they change over time whether a driver is fit to drive. A blink is fine. Eyes closed for 1.5 seconds is not.

  • Private. Your video is analysed on this device and never uploaded. The face model, about 17 MB, downloads once from jsDelivr and Google.
  • Not a safety device. This is a student engineering project. Don't rely on it to keep anyone safe.
